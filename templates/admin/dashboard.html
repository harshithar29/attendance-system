VTU Attendance Guidelines & Criteria
Statutory Minimum Attendance 75.0%

Students maintaining ≥ 75% aggregate in each subject are fully eligible for Semester End Examinations (SEE).

Condonation Margin (Medical / Sports) 65.0% - 74.9%

Condonation is subject to official principal approval upon submission of certified medical or university representation documentation.

Detention Threshold < 65.0%

Students below 65% are flagged for mandatory course repetition as per University Ordinance.
